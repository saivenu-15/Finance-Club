Club Objectives

The Finance, Economics, and E-commerce Club at RGUKT Srikakulam aims to foster knowledge, skills, and opportunities through the following objectives:

1
Enhance Knowledge

Increase members' understanding of financial markets, economic principles, and e-commerce innovations.

2
Facilitate Collaboration

Provide platforms for members to share resources and collaborate on economics and e-commerce initiatives.

3
Organize Educational Events

Host workshops, guest speaker sessions, and events to deepen understanding of financial concepts, economic trends, and e-commerce advancements.

4
Build Industry Connections

Develop partnerships with local businesses and organizations to offer real-world experiences and internship opportunities.

5
Support Entrepreneurship

Encourage members to develop e-commerce business ideas and provide guidance to transform them into viable ventures.

6
Promote Financial Responsibility

Foster personal financial management skills, including budgeting, saving, and investing.

7
Encourage Technological Exploration

Promote experimentation with new technologies and digital platforms to enhance e-commerce and digital market understanding.

8
Offer Mentorship

Provide guidance for members pursuing careers in finance, economics, or e-commerce.

9
Create an Inclusive Community

Build a welcoming environment for networking, collaboration, and mutual learning among all members.

Foundational Concepts

The club shares knowledge on key topics in Economics and E-commerce to equip members with essential skills and insights

Economics

Introduction to Supply and Demand

Understand the interaction of supply and demand in determining market prices and quantities through case studies and economic decision-making models.

Market Structures

Analyze structures like perfect competition, monopoly, and oligopoly. Study their impact on pricing, output, and market behavior using real-world examples.

Consumer Behavior and Decision-Making

Examine factors influencing consumer choices and explore behavioral economics to understand market trends.

Economic Indicators

Learn about GDP, inflation, and unemployment as measures of economic health and their role in policy-making.

International Trade and Globalization

Explore the benefits and challenges of global trade and analyze the impact of trade policies on economies.

E-commerce

Online Business Models (B2B, B2C, C2C)

Study the characteristics and strategies of different business models and understand how businesses cater to different market segments.

E-commerce Platforms and Marketplaces

Explore major platforms and their functionalities in facilitating online transactions and customer engagement.

Digital Marketing and Online Advertising

Master techniques like SEO, PPC, social media, and content marketing. Analyze successful campaigns and their impact.

Online Payment Processing and Security

Understand secure payment gateways, encryption protocols, and fraud prevention in online transactions.
